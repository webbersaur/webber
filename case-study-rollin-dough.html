Guilford, CT • Mobile Wood Fired Pizza • New site launched April 2026

Rollin Dough is Guilford's mobile wood fired pizza truck and trailer catering company. Our goal was to get them ranking for "pizza trailer in guilford" and "pizza truck in guilford" — competitive local keywords where they had zero presence.

The Challenge

Unlike Michael's Trattoria — which had nearly two decades of domain authority to build on — Rollin Dough started from absolute zero. Brand new domain. Brand new business. No existing Google reviews, no backlink profile, no search history whatsoever.

Getting a completely fresh domain onto page 1 of Google in under two weeks is the kind of result that demonstrates what proper on-page SEO, site structure, and content strategy can accomplish — even without the advantage of an established online presence.
