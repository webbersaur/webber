How It Works

Three steps, and only the first one is yours.

1

📱 You Post It

Tap a hidden button built into your own site, then say the specials out loud, type them, or photograph the board. No app, no password to remember.

2

✨ It Formats Itself

Each dish gets its own line, prices stay put, and the whole block picks up your site's fonts and colors. Long lists split into two columns so your homepage doesn't run off the screen.

3

⏳ It Comes Down On Its Own

You set when it expires. Sunday night, end of day, whenever. Nobody has to remember to take it down, so a Tuesday special never sits on your site through Friday.

    Where It Goes From There

    You typed the list once. It can go three places.

    🌐

    Onto Your Homepage

    Right where people land, styled to match the rest of your site.

    • Above the fold, not buried on a menu page
    • Long lists reflow into two tidy columns
    • Live within a minute of hitting Publish
    🖨

    Onto The Tables

    Export PDF turns the same list into a clean printed sheet - your logo up top, dishes down the middle, "Available through Sunday, August 9" at the bottom.

    • Save it as a PDF or send it straight to the printer
    • Works before you publish, so you can print a draft
    • Same list on the table as on the website, every time

    Into Your Customers' Inbox

    Publish, and the tool offers to email the same specials to your subscriber list.

    • Shows you the email and the count before it sends
    • Send a test to yourself first, or skip it entirely
    • Goes out from your own address, with an unsubscribe link
    • Only people who confirmed their email get it
