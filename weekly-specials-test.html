Weekly Specials Test Page

A safe target for the change portal's Weekly Specials feature. Publishing here touches nothing a client or customer sees.

What this page is

This is the sandbox for the Weekly Specials feature. The test-restaurant client in the change portal points at this repo, so publishing from /client/specials.html commits data/specials.json here and the band above renders it. See docs/adding-weekly-specials.md in the change-portal repo for the full runbook.

Nothing is showing above. That is the correct empty state - the band stays hidden when data/specials.json has no items, or when its expires date has passed.

Worth checking here before a real site

  • Plain strings and {name, price} objects both render (the site must tolerate either).
  • A price typed into the line stays in the line; only the separate price field renders styled.
  • Markup in a special renders as literal text, never as HTML.
  • More than six items splits into two columns.
  • An expired expires hides the whole band.
