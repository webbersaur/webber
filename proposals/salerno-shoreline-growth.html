The bar is lower than it looks. Some of these companies hold a top 3 spot with 5, 6 or 28 reviews. You don't need hundreds to compete. You need to stop being the only company in the area with zero. Your 4.9 rating on Angi shows the happy customers are already there; they just haven't been asked to leave a Google review.

What's holding Salerno back

  • No Google reviews. This is the big one. Google rarely puts a business with zero reviews in the map top 3, and homeowners skip past it even when it does show up.
  • Your Google profile doesn't name the shoreline. Its service area is set to "Fairfield" and "New Haven County" only. Branford, Guilford, Madison and North Branford aren't listed by name.
  • One category. The profile is listed only as "HVAC contractor." Competitors also list categories like heating contractor, AC repair and boiler service, so they match more searches.
  • A quiet profile. The last photos were added in March 2025, and the last post went up in September. Google favors listings that show recent activity.
  • The website is close, but stuck on page 3. Your Branford, East Haven, Guilford and Madison pages are working: Google showed them nearly 6,900 times in the last year. They sit around position 25 to 30, though, because Google trusts businesses with reviews and an active profile more. Fix the profile and the pages have room to climb.
