Section 02

Issues & Opportunities

We identified 4 areas where your GBP can be improved. These are ranked by impact on your visibility in local search results and your ability to convert searchers into customers.

1. No Review Responses

Critical

None of your 418 Google reviews have an owner response — including 20 one-star reviews. Unanswered negative reviews tell potential customers (and Google) that you don't care about feedback. Responding to reviews is one of the strongest local SEO ranking signals and builds trust with new customers who are reading reviews before visiting.

2. No Google Posts Activity

Critical

Google Posts let you publish updates, specials, events, and photos directly to your GBP listing. You're already running weekly specials (Wednesday, Thursday, Friday) but not promoting them on Google. This is a free marketing channel that signals to Google that your business is active and engaged, directly impacting your ranking in the local pack.

3. Photo Strategy Needs Consistency

Medium

While you have some owner-uploaded photos, there's no regular posting schedule. Businesses that upload new photos weekly get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks (Google data). Professional food photography with proper EXIF metadata (GPS coordinates, keywords) further boosts local relevance.

4. Weak Social Media Presence

Medium

Your Instagram (@grandapizza.north) has only 14 posts and 129 followers. No Facebook business page was found. Social signals contribute to local SEO, and these platforms are where your customers discover and share their experiences. Cross-promotion between GBP and social media amplifies both channels.
