Section 01

The Opportunity

Courtyard Restaurant is one of Old Saybrook's best-kept secrets - a quintessential diner with homemade food, fair prices, and the kind of warm, welcoming atmosphere that keeps regulars coming back. The problem? It's also one of the hardest restaurants to find. A website changes that.

No Website = Invisible Online

Critical

Right now, when someone searches "breakfast in Old Saybrook" or "lunch in Old Saybrook," the Courtyard doesn't show up in the results. Without a website, Google has almost nothing to work with. You're relying entirely on word of mouth and people happening to drive past - in a location that's already hard to find.

Hard-to-Find Location

Critical

Even people who want to visit the Courtyard struggle to find it. A website with clear visual directions, landmark photos, and an embedded map solves this. We'll create a dedicated "Find Us" experience with step-by-step visual guides so first-time visitors know exactly where to go.

Untapped Search Traffic

Opportunity

People search for breakfast and lunch spots in Old Saybrook every day. A properly optimized website can rank for these searches and put the Courtyard in front of new customers who are already hungry and looking for exactly what you offer. Your food and your hospitality do the rest.

Specials Drive Repeat Visits

Opportunity

Your breakfast and lunch specials are a major draw - but only for people who already know about them. A website with a dedicated specials page, easily updated through a simple change portal, keeps your regulars informed and gives new customers a reason to come in today.

SEO Element What We'll Do

Your Google Business Profile is often the very first thing a potential customer sees - before your website, before your menu, before anything else. When someone searches "breakfast in Old Saybrook," Google shows a map pack with business listings. The ones that rank highest are the ones that are actively managed.

Why photos matter: Businesses that regularly upload new photos to their GBP get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks. Photos of your food, your dining room, and your specials tell customers what to expect and build trust before they ever walk in.

Why review responses matter: Responding to every review - positive and negative - signals to Google that you're an active, engaged business. It's a direct local SEO ranking factor. It also shows potential customers that you care. An unanswered negative review tells people you don't listen. A thoughtful response turns it into a positive impression.

Why Google Posts matter: Google Posts let you publish your daily specials, events, and updates directly to your GBP listing. It's a free marketing channel that keeps your listing fresh and signals to Google that your business is active - which improves your ranking in local search results.
